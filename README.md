# 🌿 Crystal Bliss

### Authentic Natural Crystals & Gemstones

Crystal Bliss is a customer-facing e-commerce and product catalogue web application for discovering and purchasing authentic natural crystals, gemstones, and customised crystal products.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://crystalbliss.web.app/

---

## ✨ About Crystal Bliss

Crystal Bliss is created for people who are looking for genuine crystals and thoughtfully designed crystal products at accessible prices.

The platform showcases a curated collection of natural crystals and crystal-based products, with a focus on transparent product information, availability, pricing, and certification.

We offer products such as:

- 💎 Crystal Bracelets
- 🌙 Crystal Anklets
- 🔑 Crystal Keychains
- ✨ Custom Crystal Designs
- 💠 Individual Crystal & Gemstone Products

---

## 🛍️ What Customers Can Do

Customers can use the Crystal Bliss website to:

- Browse available crystals and gemstone products
- Explore products by category
- Search for specific crystals or products
- View product details and pricing
- Check product availability
- View certification information where applicable
- Place orders online
- Complete online payments
- Track order status
- Receive order-related updates

---

## 🌱 Our Philosophy

At Crystal Bliss, we believe crystals can be meaningful companions for mindfulness, intention, personal expression, and spiritual practices.

We respect traditional crystal symbolism while keeping our communication responsible and transparent.

Crystal Bliss does **not** make medical claims or present crystals as substitutes for professional medical treatment.

---

## 🔍 Transparency & Authenticity

We aim to provide clear and accurate information about our products, including:

- Product details
- Pricing
- Availability
- Crystal type
- Product category
- Certification information
- Order information

Our goal is to make the purchasing experience simple, transparent, and trustworthy.

---

## 📦 Ordering Experience

The customer journey is designed to be simple:

**Browse → Select → Order → Pay → Track**

Customers can explore the catalogue, select their preferred product, place an order, complete payment, and follow the progress of their order.
