# 🌿 Crystal Bliss

### Authentic Natural Crystals & Gemstones

**Authentic crystals. Thoughtful designs. Transparent information.**

Crystal Bliss is a customer-facing e-commerce and product catalogue platform for discovering and purchasing natural healing crystals, and customised crystal products.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://uk-crystalbliss.web.app/](https://uk-crystalbliss.web.app/)


## 🌿 About Crystal Bliss

Crystal Bliss is created for people looking for genuine crystals and thoughtfully designed crystal products at accessible prices.

The platform offers a curated collection of crystal products with a focus on **clear product information, transparent pricing, availability, and certification**.

### 💎 Our Collection

- 💎 Crystal Bracelets
- 🌙 Crystal Anklets
- 🔑 Crystal Keychains
- ✨ Custom Crystal Designs
- 🪨 Individual Crystals & Gemstones

## 🛍️ Customer Experience

Customers can:

- 🔎 Browse the crystal catalogue
- 🗂️ Explore products by category
- 🔍 Search for specific crystals and products
- 📋 View product details, pricing, and availability
- 📜 Access certification information where applicable
- 🛒 Place orders online
- 💳 Complete online payments
- 📦 Track order status
- 🔔 Receive order-related updates

### 🛒 Ordering Journey

**Browse → Select → Order → Pay → Track**

## 🌙 Our Philosophy

At Crystal Bliss, we believe crystals can be meaningful companions for **mindfulness, intention, personal expression, and spiritual practices**.

We respect traditional crystal symbolism while keeping our communication responsible and transparent.

> Crystal Bliss does not make medical claims or present crystals as substitutes for professional medical treatment.

---

## 🔮 Transparency & Authenticity

We aim to provide clear and accurate information about every product, including:

- 💎 Crystal type
- 📋 Product details
- 🗂️ Product category
- 💰 Pricing
- 📦 Availability
- 📜 Certification information
- 🧾 Order information

Our goal is to make discovering and purchasing crystals a **simple, transparent, and trustworthy experience**.

## 🧩 Technology

The Crystal Bliss web application is built using:

- ⚛️ React
- 📘 TypeScript
- ⚡ Vite
- 🎨 Tailwind CSS
- 🔥 Firebase
- ☁️ Firebase Cloud Functions
- 📝 Notion API
- 💳 Razorpay
- ✨ Google AI Studio
- 🚀 Antigravity
- 📜 Google Apps Script
- ⏰ Time-Based Triggers
- 📧 SMTP Email Service

---
